    Team goal trends

    How many of each team's last 10 league games (home and away) went over 2.5 or had both teams score. Teams with fewer than 10 show it as, say, 4/6. Tap a team to see its last 20 games.

    How the numbers are made

    1. Every team gets attack and defence ratings for home and away games, measured against the league average.
    2. Ratings blend goals scored with shots on target, converted to goals at the league's average rate.
    3. A game from about 16 months ago counts half as much as one played this week.
    4. Teams with few games, such as newly promoted sides, are pulled toward the league average.
    5. Expected goals for both sides give a total, and a Poisson distribution turns that into the chance of 3 or more goals.
